LABORATORY PROTOCOL — Candidate 1 (T6)
==========================================================================

1. TARGET COMPOSITION (wt%)
   Al:87.10  Mg:4.66  Zn:7.07  Cu:1.15  Si:0.01  Fe:0.01

2. CHARGE for a 500 g ingot (with overage)
   Al    435.50 g
   Zn     37.81 g
   Mg     25.61 g
   Cu      5.76 g
   Si      0.06 g
   Fe      0.06 g
   (balance = Al, add last to minimise oxidation)

3. MELTING & CASTING
   Melt at 700 C; pour at 690 C into a steel mould.
   Add Al-5Ti-1B grain refiner at 0.1 wt% just before pouring.

4. HOMOGENISATION (optional but recommended)
   465 C / 12 h, then air cool — removes microsegregation.

5. SOLUTION TREATMENT
   465-475 C for 2 h
   Quench: cold water (<40 C), transfer < 10 s
   (stay BELOW 477 C to avoid incipient melting)

6. ARTIFICIAL AGING
   T6 (peak-aged): 120 C for 24 h, then air cool

7. PREDICTED PROPERTIES (model + conformal 90% interval)
   Yield strength : 625 MPa  [518, 733]
   UTS (est.)     : 674 MPa
   Elongation     : 8.9 %
   Density        : 2.773 g/cm3

8. CHARACTERISATION CHECKLIST
   [ ] Optical/SEM + EDS  — grain size, second-phase morphology
   [ ] XRD                — confirm fcc Al matrix (+ MgZn2 / Al2Cu peaks)
   [ ] Vickers HV (10 indent) — before and after ageing
   [ ] Tensile test, 3 specimens, ASTM E8 — YS, UTS, elongation
   [ ] Density (Archimedes) — compare with prediction

9. NOTES
   - Zn & Mg overcharged (Zn +7%, Mg +10%) to offset volatilisation losses.
   - Do NOT exceed 475 C in solutionising: incipient melting of the Al-Zn-Mg-Cu eutectic occurs near 477 C -> hot cracking.
   - Grain refinement: add Al-5Ti-1B master alloy (0.1 wt%) before pouring.
   - Degas with Ar or C2Cl6 to limit porosity (H2 solubility falls sharply on solidification).
   - Cu-rich: expect Al2Cu/Al7Cu2Fe dispersoids; slow cooling may form coarse constituent phases -> minimise with rapid solidification.
